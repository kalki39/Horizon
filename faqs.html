Frequently Asked Question

  • How do I register for a course/program?

    You can easily register by filling out the enrollment form on our website. Once submitted, our team will contact you to guide you through the next steps of the admission process.

  • Do I need any prior experience before joining?

    Not at all. Our programs are designed for beginners as well as experienced learners. You will be guided step by step.

  • Are classes conducted online or offline?

    We provide both online and offline options. You can choose the mode of learning that suits you best.

  • What kind of support will I get during the course?

    You will receive mentorship, doubt-clearing sessions, and continuous guidance throughout your learning journey.

  • Will I receive a certificate after completion?

    Yes, upon successfully completing the course, you will be awarded a certificate that can be used for career opportunities.

  • Can I access study materials after the course ends?

    Yes, you will continue to have access to all study materials, notes, and resources provided during the training. This will help you revise and refresh your knowledge anytime in the future.

  • Do you provide placement assistance?

    Yes, we offer placement guidance, resume preparation, and mock interview support to help you in your career.

  • Can I pay the fees in installments??

    Yes, flexible payment plans and EMI options are available to make it easier for students.

  • Who can I contact if I have more questions?

    You can reach out to our support team via phone, email, or live chat. We’re always happy to help.

  • Do I need a medical or life science background to join?

    Not at all! While life science graduates have an advantage, even freshers from other streams can start and succeed with our structured training.
