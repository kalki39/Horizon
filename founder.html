Our Visionary Leader

Leading the Future of Medical Coding Education

Founded in 2019 by Ms. Hannah Ponnarasi, our institute is built on the vision and expertise of a seasoned professional with over 9 years of hands-on coding experience and 5 years of leadership in shaping the next generation of coders. Recognizing the immense potential and career opportunities in the medical coding industry, Hannah set out to create a learning platform that blends global standards with personalized mentorship.

What began as a single training center with a handful of passionate learners has now grown into a recognized hub for quality coding education, attracting students from across the country and beyond. Over the years, we have successfully trained and certified hundreds of aspiring coders, many of whom are now thriving in rewarding healthcare careers.

Driven by a commitment to excellence, innovation, and student success, we continue to raise the bar in medical coding training—preparing professionals who are not only skilled but also industry-ready from day one.
