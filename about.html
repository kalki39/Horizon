About Us

Horizon Coding Solutions was founded in 2013 with a clear vision – to empower students, graduates, and healthcare professionals with the knowledge and skills to excel in the medical coding industry. Over the years, we have grown into one of the most trusted names in medical coding education, known for our quality training, placement support, and commitment to student success.

Leadership Behind Horizon Coding Solutions

Shakshi Roy – Co-Founder & Director

With over a decade of experience in the healthcare and medical coding sector, Shakshi Roy laid the foundation of Horizon Coding Solutions in 2013. As Co-Founder and Director, she envisioned creating an academy that not only imparts technical knowledge but also instills confidence, discipline, and career readiness in every student. .

She has built Horizon into a platform that bridges the gap between academic learning and real-world industry requirements. Her passion lies in helping students discover career opportunities in medical coding, guiding them toward global certifications, and ensuring they are well-prepared for placement in leading companies.

Shakshi’s vision: “Education should not just train you for a job – it should prepare you for growth, ethics, and excellence in your career.”
Hannah Ponnarasi, Training and business development head

As the Training and Business Development Head, Hannah Ponnarasi.X brings immense value to Horizon through her expertise in training, placement, and operational assistance. She plays a dual role – shaping the academic curriculum to match industry standards and creating strong placement networks to help students secure opportunities in reputed healthcare organizations.

Her focus on hands-on training, one-on-one mentorship, and career guidance ensures that students are not just certified coders but confident professionals ready to thrive in competitive work environments. She also oversees business development, building partnerships and expanding Horizon’s reach across India.

Hannah’s belief: “Every student has the potential to succeed – they just need the right guidance, training, and opportunities to shine.”

Our Story

A Better Future Starts Here

Horizon Coding Solutions was founded with one clear vision: to make medical coding education accessible, affordable, and career-focused. In a world where healthcare is rapidly evolving, we realized the need for skilled medical coders who can bridge the gap between patient care and healthcare management.

Building Education for the Future

We designed our training programs with students and professionals in mind—keeping them practical, flexible, and aligned with global healthcare standards. With a focus on ICD-10, CPT, and HCPCS coding systems, our courses prepare learners for medical coding certification exams while providing real-world case studies that reflect industry practices.

A Journey to Excellence
